## 4. Key Site-Wide Technical & On-Page Findings

### Strengths Identified Across the Website
1. **100% Dynamic Header & Meta Inclusion**: Every page calls `includes/header.php`, guaranteeing:
   - Unique `<title>` tag on 1,035 / 1,035 pages.
   - Unique `<meta name="description">` on 1,035 / 1,035 pages.
   - Dynamic clean `<link rel="canonical">` stripping `.php` extensions and `pages/` directory prefixes.
   - Open Graph (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`, `og:type`) & Twitter Cards.
2. **Strict Heading Hierarchy**: Every page contains exactly one `<h1>` heading (1,035 / 1,035 pages), preventing heading dilution.
3. **Advanced Multi-Entity Structured Data**:
   - `MovingCompany` schema with dynamic city geo-coordinates, street address, rating, payment accepted, and offer catalog.
   - `BreadcrumbList` schema mapping hierarchy (Home -> City Hub -> Target Locality/Route).
   - `WebPage` schema.
   - `FAQPage` schema automatically populated whenever `$faq_list` is defined on a page.
4. **Rich Interlinking Matrix**: Hub pages and locality pages link to neighboring localities, major district hubs, and interstate routes.

### Technical & Content Weaknesses Identified
1. **Content Density Variance**:
   - **427 pages** (41.3%) meet or exceed the top-tier 1,800+ word count mandate.
   - **608 pages** (58.7%) fall between 800 and 1,750 words (legacy route templates).
2. **Static Asset Caching Dependency**: While `.htaccess` sets 1-year browser caching, external Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`) and static assets could be further optimized with local font hosting.
