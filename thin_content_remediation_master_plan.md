## Core Guiding Principles & Constraints
1. **11-Section Master Content Blueprint**: Mandatory application of all 11 standardized sections on every page.
2. **Zero-Deletion & Zero-Redirect Policy**: Every 200 OK PHP asset is kept live and remediated manually.
3. **Canonical Alignment**: Sub-locality pages explicitly declare canonical URLs pointing to their respective District Master Hubs.
4. **Manual Execution**: Strict manual ("one-page-at-a-time") editing protocol; zero automated bulk scripts.
